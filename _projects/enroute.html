En route is a 2-4 player strategy racing board game set in the train network of India. In the game, players play as passengers, make plans and strategies to travel to different places, catch, board, and de-board different trains while also managing to gather and exchange train information, exploit resources, pull chains to stop trains, negotiate with other players, discover new destinations, and visit their favorite places across the country. En route is an enriching playing and learning experience for all kinds of players, from beginners to those who enjoy playing strategy train games.

Game board


The map is an abstract representation of real railway routes and stations of India; it has more than 85 major stations across the country. There are hundreds of trains running on different lines, and players with the correct train information and a ticket can board the train and travel to different places. Every station has a colored hex placed on top of it; there are five categories of stations depending on the number of trains and connecting routes. Players travel across the board and collect these hexes, which mean different things at different points during the journey. The hexes score points except black; black doesn't score any points but can be used as any other color as players wish.

Game Setup


At the start of the game, each player picks a station card from the station card deck and places it face up revealing their starting location. All players then get three station cards, which become their immediate destinations to cover. Each player gets five movement cards, which they use to move and navigate on the board every turn. The movement deck consists of two types of cards: one is a train card deck featuring over 12 types of trains with information about the train and the speed at which it runs; along with a few special cards like chain pulling, flying, and rolling a die, shuffled together to make a deck of more than 120 movement cards. Players get two empty luggage boxes each and keep them in front of them throughout the game to collect the colored hexes.




movement and station cards

Gameplay


Each turn, players draw two movement cards from the deck and can play one. As players travel across the board, they collect different colored hexes and store them in their luggage box. As players reach a destination and finish one of their station cards, they place a marker there revealing it to the other players. Players at the same station can exchange train information and can also decide to trade hexes if they wish. Different colored hexes score different points, and special sets or combinations of hexes award bonus points as shown on the reference sheet.


As players try to get the perfect combination of hexes and reach their destinations, if at any point any player finishes all their destination cards and also fills both luggage boxes with a complete set of any value, it triggers the game end. After the final round, all players calculate their respective scores, and the player with the highest score wins the game, irrespective of who triggered the end.
