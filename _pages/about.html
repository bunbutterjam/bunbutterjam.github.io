Swapnesh is a designer and researcher of games. He is a faculty member at the National Insitute of Design, India in the Desecipline of Digital Game Design.

In the past, he worked as an independent designer and consultant for startups and NGOs in the area of Game Design, Playful Learning and Craft Research.

In my spare time, I volunteer with play and learning centers in conducting various workshops, and developing playful learning tools and material for teachers and homeschoolers.
