Hi, I'm Swapnesh! I make and study games. I'm a faculty member at the National Institute of Design, India in the Discipline of Digital Game Design.

I have designed and developed 15+ digital and tabletop games for various clients and communities across India. I'm interested in exploring games and related ludic forms as a cultural practice, from a perspective of performance, poetry, social and aesthetic experiences.

In the past, I have worked as an independent designer and consultant for startups, NGOs, homeschoolers and design institutions in the area of Game Design, Playful Learning, Gamification, Interaction Design and Craft Research.
